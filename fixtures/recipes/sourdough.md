# Sourdough Bread

## Ingredients
- 500g bread flour
- 350g water (70% hydration)
- 100g active starter
- 10g salt

## Steps
1. Mix flour and water, autolyse for 30 minutes.
2. Add starter and salt, mix until incorporated.
3. Bulk ferment 4-5 hours at room temperature, folding every 30 minutes for the first 2 hours.
4. Shape and place in a banneton, retard overnight in the fridge.
5. Bake at 250C in a covered Dutch oven for 20 minutes, then uncovered at 230C for 20-25 minutes.
