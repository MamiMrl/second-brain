# Red Lentil Soup

## Ingredients
- 1 cup red lentils
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 carrots, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 4 cups vegetable stock
- Juice of half a lemon

## Steps
1. Saute onion and carrots until soft, about 5 minutes.
2. Add garlic, cumin, and turmeric, cook 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Add lentils and stock, bring to a boil, then simmer 20 minutes until lentils are soft.
4. Blend partially for a creamy texture, finish with lemon juice and salt to taste.
